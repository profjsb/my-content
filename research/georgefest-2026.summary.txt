In this 15-minute tribute at GeorgeFest (Caltech, September 2026), Bloom tells the story of the gamma-ray burst afterglow era as he lived it in Caltech's GRB group from 1997, drawing on thirty years of emails with George Djorgovski: George refereeing (and rejecting) his only rejected paper, on GRB offsets from host galaxies, just before Shri Kulkarni recruited him on the boat back from the 1997 Elba workshop; the fast-moving 970508 redshift circular and Bloom's Nature News and Views on the end of the great debate; keeping the record z=3.42 redshift of GRB 971214 secret; the jet break of GRB 990510, host-galaxy offsets that disfavored merging neutron stars for long GRBs, and the 1998 supernova bump plus an unpublished Keck spectrum that anticipated the 2003 GRB–supernova confirmation. He closes with a Claude-assisted census of their 38 joint papers and a handful of George's saltier emails, and credits George with teaching him to be a spectroscopist.
