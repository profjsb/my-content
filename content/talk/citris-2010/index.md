Research Exchange lecture on taking the astronomer out of the real-time loop: robotic telescopes that talk to each other over VOEvent, discovery engines like the Palomar Transient Factory that will soon outrun follow-up resources, and machine-learned real-bogus scoring and classification of transients and variable stars.

*A noon talk in CITRIS's Research Exchange series (Sutardja Dai Hall), introduced by Masoud Nikravesh, CITRIS's Director for Computational Science and Engineering, and webcast to the other CITRIS campuses. The title shown is descriptive: the announced title was not preserved, and the slide deck survives only as an empty file. CITRIS's recording is linked above.*
