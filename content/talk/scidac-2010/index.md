*Listed in the meeting's schedule under its assigned title, "Present and Future Transient/Supernovae Studies in the Infrared" (Thursday, May 20, 4:30–4:55pm, in "Observations and Searches – Mission Planning"), at the consortium's May 19–21 meeting at SLAC.*
