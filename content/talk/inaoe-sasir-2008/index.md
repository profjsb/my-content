Opening overview of the Synoptic All-Sky Infrared survey, a proposed 6.5-meter telescope at San Pedro Mártir that would map the whole sky in four near-infrared bands every few months, with the questions and decision timeline for the workshop.

*He spoke three times at the nine-day workshop at INAOE: this overview on Wednesday, August 13, a revised overview on August 18 ([slides](https://www.inaoep.mx/~progharo/gh2008/Bloom_2.pdf)) and a talk on detectors on August 19 ([slides](https://www.inaoep.mx/~progharo/gh2008/Bloomdetectors.pdf)).*
