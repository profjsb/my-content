Conference talk arguing that gamma-ray bursts' long/short split no longer maps cleanly onto their progenitors (durations, spectral lags and host offsets all have exceptions) and proposing a physically based classification instead.

*The written version is the proceedings paper ["Gamma-ray Bursts, Classified Physically"](/publication/2008-aipc-1000-11-b/) (Bloom, Butler & Perley 2008).*
