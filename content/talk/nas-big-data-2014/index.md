Data literacy before big-data proficiency: what Berkeley's Python boot camps and graduate seminar showed about training domain scientists, and the open questions — where such courses fit in the curriculum, who teaches them and how they're credited, the 'novelty-squared' problem, and inclusion.

*Given April 11, 2014, as the first talk in the workshop's session on courses, curricula, and interdisciplinary programs (chaired by James Frew, with talks by Peter Fox and Bill Howe). The National Academies Press proceedings summarize it in [Chapter 4](https://nap.nationalacademies.org/read/18981/chapter/5).*
