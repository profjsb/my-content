Benjamin Dean public lecture in the Morrison Planetarium on SN 2011fe, the nearest Type Ia supernova in decades, caught hours after explosion by the Palomar Transient Factory's machine-learning pipeline: what it revealed about the exploding white dwarf and its companion, and why Type Ia supernovae matter for cosmology.

*His second Benjamin Dean lecture in the Morrison Planetarium (the [first](/talk/dean-lecture-2010/) was in 2010), introduced by Ryan Wyatt, the planetarium's director, who also moderated the questions. The Academy's audio recording (1 hr 24 min, from its former iTunes U collection) is linked above; the slides' title page misdates the lecture to September 9.*
